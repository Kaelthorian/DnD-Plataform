# College of Mercantile Manual Testing

1. Create or load a Bard.
2. At level 2, confirm College of Mercantile grants no subclass features.
3. At level 3, select College of Mercantile and confirm Coin Flip and Magic Coin appear.
4. Confirm Magic Coin appears in the action/attack panel, uses the spell attack bonus, and rolls 1 + 1d12 damage.
5. At level 6, confirm Money Talks appears and Magic Coin rolls 1 + 2d12 damage.
6. Confirm Money Talks shows uses equal to Charisma modifier, minimum 1, and refreshes on long rest as a rules reminder.
7. At level 14, confirm Shrewd Consumer appears and Magic Coin rolls 1 + 3d12 damage.
8. Confirm existing Bard subclasses still appear and their features still load.
